Why I built this

I'm currently a second-year medical student. Anatomy is a defining part of the medical school curriculum, especially if your school uses cadavers. Anki is an invaluable resource, but I found it to be an inefficient way to study for anatomy. The inspiration for this site came from a few online quizzes that made it easy to learn structures quickly. There weren't many of them, and the images were very low quality.

So I wanted quizzes like that for the entire anatomy curriculum, with high-quality images.

How the images are made

I used Z-Anatomy, an open 3D atlas of the human body, to build a database of body parts and the connections between them. Sort of like the human atlas project, but not as serious. Then I wrote a program in Blender that renders images using that database. An imaging engine adds the finer surface detail (muscle fibers, tendon, the texture of bone). I review each one before it goes on the site.

See a mistake or want me to add something?

I have tried to personally verify the accuracy of every image – but I am only HUMAN. If you see an error, let me know and I will get it fixed. To get this cool free resource out there expeditiously, it will remain a work in progress, and I will continue to add more systems and anatomical groups until complete. Suggestions and feedback are welcome.
