Why I built this

I'm a medical student. Anki is an invaluable resource for medical students, but for anatomy I found it super slow. The inspiration for this site came from a few online quizzes that made it really easy to learn structures quickly. There just weren't many of them, and the images were very low quality.

I wanted quizzes like that for the entire anatomy curriculum, with high-quality images.

How the images are made

I used Z-Anatomy, an open 3D atlas of the human body, to build a database of body parts and how they connect. I then wrote a program in Blender that renders anatomically correct images from that model, one group of structures at a time.

An image model adds the finer surface detail on top of each render: muscle fibers, tendon, the texture of bone. Every finished image is checked against the original render so that no structure moves or changes shape, and I review each one before it goes on the site.

See a mistake?

I have tried to personally verify the accuracy of every image. If you see an error, let me know and I will get it fixed.
